# Future Scope

Decisions deferred from v1, with context for why and when to revisit.

---

## Parsing

### Hybrid Form 16 Parser (Rule-based + LLM)
**Current:** Full LLM extraction (Claude) for both Part A and Part B of Form 16.

**Why defer:** LLM extraction is more robust for v1 — handling unknown employer formats without maintenance overhead justifies the cost at low volume.

**Future approach:**
- Part A (TAN, PAN, TDS deducted/deposited) — generated by TRACES, always standardized → pure `pdfplumber` regex, no LLM needed
- Part B (salary breakdown, deductions) — varies by employer → LLM only for this section
- Cuts LLM token usage by ~40%, same accuracy

**When to revisit:** When filing volume crosses ~500/month and per-filing API cost becomes material.

---

### Scanned / Image-based Form 16
**Current:** Assumes digitally generated PDFs (text-selectable).

**Future:** Add OCR preprocessing step (Tesseract or AWS Textract) before LLM extraction for scanned PDFs.

## Tax Computation

### ~~Senior Citizen Slabs~~ ✓ Done
DOB input added. Correct slab (regular / senior 60–79 / super senior 80+) selected based on age at any time during the FY (March 31 end-date rule). Both old and new regime handled.

### ~~Section 80D — Parents' Health Insurance~~ ✓ Done
Parents tier added: ₹25,000 cap (₹50,000 if senior parents). Manual entry. Self/family cap (₹25,000) comes from Form 16. Total max ₹75,000.

### ~~80GG — Rent Deduction Without HRA~~ ✓ Done
Auto-detected when HRA component = 0. Same rent inputs as HRA, different formula. Mutually exclusive with HRA exemption.

### ~~Home Loan Interest via Bank Certificate~~ ✓ Done
Manual override field in Review step. Takes precedence over Form 16 value. Capped at ₹2L. Let-out property has separate loan interest field (no cap).

### ~~80EEA — Affordable Housing Additional Deduction~~ ✓ Done
Manual entry. Engine caps at ₹1.5L, zeroes in new regime and when let-out property is active. CA verifies eligibility conditions (stamp duty ≤ ₹45L, loan Apr 2019–Mar 2022, first-time buyer).

### Capital Gains, Business Income
**Current:** ITR-1 only — salaried individuals with no capital gains or business income.

**Future:** Support ITR-2 (capital gains, foreign assets) and ITR-3/4 (business/profession income). Requires significant expansion of the tax engine and a separate form generator.

### F&O (Futures & Options) — P3
**Current:** Out of scope.

**Why it's different from capital gains:** Section 43(5) classifies F&O as non-speculative business income, not capital gains. Filers with any F&O activity must use ITR-3, regardless of how small the F&O turnover is. ITR-2 is not sufficient.

**Key mechanics:**
- Turnover = absolute sum of all trade-level profits and losses (not net P&L). Used to determine audit requirement.
- Tax audit (Section 44AB): required if F&O turnover > ₹10Cr, or if a loss is declared and turnover > ₹1Cr under presumptive tax scheme.
- F&O losses can be carried forward for 8 years and set off only against business income (not salary or capital gains).
- Business expenses (brokerage, STT partially, internet, advisory fees) are deductible against F&O income.

**Data source:** Broker annual tax P&L report (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Angel One provide PDFs or Excel downloads). Carry-forward losses require prior year ITR acknowledgement — manual entry.

**Prerequisite:** ITR-3 generator (P3 — not started).

## ITR Filing

### ERI Integration (Direct API Filing)
**Current:** Generates ITR-1 JSON for manual upload to the IT portal.

**Future:** Register as an ERI (e-Return Intermediary) with the Income Tax Department to file programmatically on behalf of taxpayers via the official API. This removes the manual upload step entirely and enables bulk filing.

**When to revisit:** Once the product has validated demand and the CA relationship is established.

### Form 26AS / AIS Auto-fetch
**Current:** TDS figures taken from Form 16 only.

**Future:** Fetch Form 26AS and AIS (Annual Information Statement) from the IT portal to cross-verify TDS and flag mismatches before filing.

## Infrastructure

### Multi-employer / Multiple Form 16s
**Current:** Single Form 16 per return.

**Future:** Handle job changes mid-year — merge two Form 16s (two employers), sum TDS, reconcile salary figures.

### CA Dashboard
**Current:** Python pipeline only.

**Future:** Web UI for CAs to upload Form 16 PDFs, review extracted data, approve/edit, and download the ITR JSON or trigger direct filing.
